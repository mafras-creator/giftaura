# GiftAura — Customer Guide

How to shop on the GiftAura website. Website address on the development computer: `http://localhost/giftaura/`

---

## 1. Create an account

1. Click **Create account** (top right).
2. Enter your full name, e-mail, mobile number (e.g. `0771234567`) and a password (at least 8 characters with a letter and a number).
3. Click **Create account**. You are logged in straight away.
4. **Verify your e-mail address.** We send you an e-mail *"Please verify your e-mail address"*. Open it and press **Verify my e-mail address**, then **Confirm**. The link works once and for 24 hours. Until you do this a yellow reminder is shown and you cannot check out (you can still browse and fill your cart).
   - No e-mail? Check the **Spam** folder, then press **Send the link again** in the yellow reminder (up to 3 times per hour).

You can look at products and fill your cart without an account; you need one to place an order.

## 2. Find a gift

- **Categories**: click a round category picture on the homepage, open **All categories** below the purple bar at the top, or use the left column of the **Shop** page. Subcategories appear under their category.
- **Search**: type in the white search box in the purple bar at the top (e.g. `roses`) and press Enter or the yellow search button.
- **Filter**: on the Shop page set a price range, tick **In stock only**, then **Apply filters**. **Clear filters** removes them.
- **Sort by**: newest, price low → high, price high → low, or name.
- Product labels: **Only 3 left** = low stock; **Out of stock** = cannot be ordered at the moment.

## 2b. Offers and wishlist

- **On sale**: products with a red **-%** badge are discounted; the old price is crossed out and you pay the new price. Click **On sale** in the menu to see all offers.
- **Wishlist**: press the **heart** on any product picture to save it for later (you need to be logged in; if not, you are asked to log in and the product is saved straight after). Press the heart again to remove it. See all saved products under **Wishlist** at the top, or **My account → My wishlist**.

## 2c. Personalised gifts

Products marked **Personalisable** can carry your own name or message.
1. Press **Personalise** on the product (or open the product).
2. Type your text in the **Personalise it** box — the counter shows how many characters are left. Use **Enter** for a new line. Check the spelling: it is printed exactly as typed.
3. **Add to cart**. In the cart you can **Edit personalisation** until you place the order.
4. One message per product: if you add the same product again with a new message, the new message replaces the old one (for two different names, place two orders or write both names in one message if the product allows it).
5. The message appears on your order confirmation, in **My orders** and in the order e-mail.

## 2d. Reviews

- Every product page has **Customer reviews**: the average rating, how many customers gave each number of stars, and their comments. Only customers who received the product can review it ("Verified purchase").
- After your order is **delivered**, open **My account → My reviews** (or the link in the "delivered" e-mail, or **Write a review** on the order page), choose 1–5 stars, write a comment if you like, and press **Post review**.
- Only your first name and the first letter of your last name are shown. You can **edit or delete** your review at any time from My reviews.

## 3. Add to cart

You need an account to use the cart. When you press **Add to cart** without being logged in, you are asked to log in or create a free account — the product you chose (with your personalisation message) is then put in your cart automatically and you come back to the page you were on, so you can keep shopping.

- Click the yellow **Add to cart** button on a product card, or open the product, choose the quantity with **−** / **+** and click **Add to cart**.
- The **Cart** link at the top shows how many items you have.
- In the cart you can change a quantity (**Update**) or **Remove** an item. Prices and stock are checked again every time the cart opens; if something is no longer available, the cart tells you what to change.
- The cart shows **Subtotal**, **Delivery** and **Total** in Sri Lankan rupees.


## 4. Check out

1. In the cart click **Proceed to checkout** (log in if asked).
2. **Your contact details** — name and mobile.
3. **Delivery address** — choose a saved address or **Deliver to a new address** (tick *Save this address to my account* to reuse it). Add an optional **gift message or delivery note**.
4. **Payment**:
   - **Cash on delivery** — pay the courier when the gift arrives.
   - **Bank transfer** — transfer the total to the store's account; the bank details are shown after you place the order. No card details are ever asked for.
5. Check the total and click **Place order**.
6. You get an **order reference** such as `GA261016-7K3M9Q`. Keep it. We also e-mail you a confirmation with the items, total and delivery address.

If a product sold out or a price changed while you were checking out, the order is **not** placed and you are shown the updated cart — you are never charged a different amount from the one you saw.

## 5. Paying by bank transfer

1. Transfer the exact total, using your order reference as the payment reference.
2. Open **My account → My orders → your order**.
3. Type the reference from your bank slip in **Transfer reference** and click **Send reference**.
4. The payment shows **Awaiting verification** until the store checks its bank account, then **Paid**. If it shows **Failed**, check the details and send the reference again, or contact the store.

## 6. Follow your order

- **My account → My orders** lists every order with its status and payment status. Open an order to see items, totals, delivery address and a progress bar: **Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Ready for delivery → Delivered**.
- Without logging in: **Track your order** (bottom of every page) → enter the order reference and the e-mail you used. For your privacy, only the status is shown.

## 7. Cancel an order

You can cancel while the order is still **Pending**: open the order → **Cancel order** (optionally give a reason) → confirm. Once the store has confirmed the order, please contact the store instead.

## 8. Your account

| Menu | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Change your name and mobile number (your e-mail is your login and cannot be changed here) |
| **My orders** | See and track orders, cancel pending ones, send bank references |
| **Addresses** | Add, edit, delete addresses and choose your default (up to 10) |
| **Change password** | Enter your current password and a new one |

**Forgotten password?** On the **Log in** page click **Forgotten your password?**, enter your e-mail and press **Send reset link**. Open the e-mail *"Reset your GiftAura password"* within 60 minutes and choose a new password. If no e-mail arrives (check Spam), contact the store and staff can give you a temporary password.

**E-mails you receive:** e-mail verification, password reset (only when you ask), order received, order confirmed / being prepared / ready for delivery / delivered / cancelled, and payment received / refunded. Every e-mail has a **View my order** button.

You are logged out automatically after 30 minutes without activity. Always **Log out** on a shared computer.

## 9. Questions

Open **Contact** → *Send us a message*: choose what it is about, add your order reference if you have one, and write your message. You get a reference (INQ-0001) and our reply by e-mail. If you were logged in, your messages and our replies are also in **My account → My inquiries**. Please never send card or bank passwords. The Contact page also shows the store's phone, e-mail, address and opening hours when they are published.
